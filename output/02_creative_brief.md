## The one-line strategy

Run Scribe's winning UGC formula, "you do the task, the documentation takes care of itself", and upgrade the output from a written doc to an AI video guide, so the ad also beats the objection Scribe ignores: *nobody reads the doc anyway.*

## Who it is for

Cold prospecting audience (matches the winner). The person who owns onboarding, enablement, support answers, or SOPs, and personally loses hours turning "how I do this" into something a teammate can follow. They are not shopping for software; they are quietly resentful of a recurring chore. The ad names that chore.

## What we want them to feel and do

- **Feel:** "That is my exact Tuesday. Wait, that is not a thing you have to do anymore?"
- **Do:** tap **Create your first Guidde free.**

## Message hierarchy

1. **Hook (the pain, no product):** the dread of making the how-to, not the task itself.
2. **Angle (the reframe):** you do the task once; Guidde turns it into a polished step-by-step video guide on its own.
3. **The Guidde edge:** it is a *video*, so people watch it instead of skipping a wall of text, and stop asking you the same question.
4. **Proof:** one hard number (placeholder until a real Guidde stat is dropped in): hours saved per week, or repeat questions deflected.
5. **CTA:** Create your first Guidde free.

## Hook options (pick one to shoot, A/B the rest)

- "POV: you spent your whole morning writing a how-to nobody will read." *(closest to the winner)*
- "The part nobody warns you about in this job: explaining the same task for the hundredth time."
- "I record my screen once and I never write another how-to again."
- "Stop writing SOPs. Nobody reads them anyway."

## Voice and tone

First-person creator, casual, a little fed-up-then-relieved. Spoken like a tip you are giving a friend, not ad copy. No jargon, no feature names in the first half. Sound-on script must stay fully legible as burned-in captions with sound off.

## Visual direction

- **UGC, not brand film.** One real person, handheld/selfie framing, an ordinary real location (desk, kitchen, car), natural light. It has to pass as organic in-feed.
- **9:16 vertical**, large high-contrast captions, one line at a time.
- **One product beat only:** a clean screen recording of Guidde turning a captured workflow into a video guide (the "it builds itself" moment). It appears as the payoff, never a feature tour.
- **Brand appears late,** on the end card only, so the hook stays un-branded and keeps the watch.

## Brand and copy guardrails

- Clean modern sans for on-screen type (Inter or similar). No trendy display serifs.
- Whole-sentence captions, no two-word fragment headlines.
- No em dashes in any on-screen copy.
- Neutral, real palette through the story; a single confident Guidde-purple pop reserved for the end card.

## Deliverables

- 1 hero cut, 9:16, about 30 seconds, sound-on and muted-legible.
- Cutdowns: 15s and 6s (hook + CTA) for autoplay and retargeting.
- Storyboard and shot list: see [03_storyboard.md](03_storyboard.md).

## The bet

The winner proves the market rewards "the documentation takes care of itself," delivered by a real creator, as a relatable chore they can retire. Guidde can run that exact ad and add the payoff Scribe structurally cannot promise: the guide is a video, so it actually gets watched.
