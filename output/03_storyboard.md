## The full ad (not just the video)

- **Hook (on-screen, 0:00):** "Being the one who knows how everything works is a second full-time job."
- **Primary text (post caption):** The part nobody warns you about when you're the one who knows how everything works: you become the office help desk, rewriting the same SOP nobody reads. It doesn't have to be that way. Guidde captures any workflow and turns it into a professional step-by-step video, with an AI voiceover, in 40+ languages, that you share across your whole team with one link. You do your job, the guide does the explaining. Start free at guidde.com
- **CTA:** Try For Free
- **End card:** the maroon "g" / Try For Free slide.

## Direction notes
- **Two people, one car.** Driver = the problem, passenger = the fix. Keep it fast and dry, no dead air between them (that mirrors the winner and reads as authentic, not scripted).
- **One product beat only (shot 3).** The real Guidde platform appears exactly once, as the payoff to the complaint, over the passenger's line, never a feature tour.
- **Features are Guidde's real ones** (checked on guidde.com): capture any workflow, AI voiceover, 40+ languages, share with one link. SOPs kept (Guidde's own use case); Scribe's "tedious calls" language deliberately dropped.
- **Legible muted.** Big burned-in subtitles on every line for sound-off autoplay.
- **Grounding:** the winner it adapts is Scribe's car-POV UGC monologue (Meta Library ID 1737809570795386).
