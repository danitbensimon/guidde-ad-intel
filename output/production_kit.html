3 rules that make or break it

  • Light British accent, both women. Casual, flat, tired delivery, no acting, no ad polish.
  • Generate each shot separately (~5s, Kling 3.0, 9:16), then hard-cut them together, no gaps.

Putting it together

  1. Generate shots 1–5 in Kling 3.0, 9:16, ~5s each. Re-roll until the audio sounds right — you can hear it, that's your edge.
  2. Drop them in order in an editor (CapCut). Hard cuts, no gaps between shots — that kills the awkward pauses.
  3. Trim any dead air inside a clip (the silence between the two speakers).
  4. Add your Guidde screen recording as the product cut inside shot 3.
  5. Add captions (CapCut → Auto captions), then fix any word so the brand reads Guidde.
  6. Add the maroon end card at the end.
